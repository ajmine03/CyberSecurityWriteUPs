## About & Disclaimer

### Purpose

This documentation base serves as an ongoing log of practical security research, hands-on lab experiments, and competitive CTF problem solving. Notes emphasize core concepts, attack methodologies, and repeatable workflows rather than raw solutions.

### Responsible Disclosure & Ethics

All writeups and notes are published for educational and authorized penetration testing purposes only. No unauthorized access is promoted or condoned. Laboratory exercises and challenges are completed within designated, legal environments (such as TryHackMe and Hack The Box).
